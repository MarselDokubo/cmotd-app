The Centre for Marine and Offshore Technology Development (CMOTD) is a specialized technical institution and research centre established in 2021 within Rivers State University in Port Harcourt, Nigeria. It functions as a premier hub for advanced engineering education, specialized training, and research tailored specifically to the maritime, shipping, and offshore oil and gas industries. cmotd.org

**Key aspects of CMOTD include:**
_Core Mission & Vision:_ The centre aims to bridge the gap between academia and industry by developing highly skilled postgraduate engineering manpower. It focuses on fostering innovation, technology transfer, and sustainable practices for the responsible exploitation of offshore oil and gas, renewable energy, and other ocean resources.

_Industrial Training & Professional Certifications:_ Beyond traditional degree pathways, CMOTD provides wide-ranging professional short courses and technical certifications. These include training for industry-standard software and operational competencies such as:

- Process simulation and engineering design tools (HYSYS, PV Elite, Caesar II for pipe stress analysis, AVEVA PDMS)
- Maritime security and compliance (ISPS Code, Integrated Management Systems, Lead Marine Auditor)
- Specialized practical skills including Marine Surveying, Rigging & Scaffolding, Class Welding & Fabrication, Flow Metering & Instrument Calibration, and Solar Engineering Development.

_Facilities & Collaboration:_ The centre is equipped with modern infrastructure, including marine hydrodynamics laboratories, computing and IT facilities for specialized software modeling, and frameworks designed to facilitate active collaboration between university researchers and industry professionals.
