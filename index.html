Executive Editorial & Vision

"Bridging academic rigor with high-stakes Gulf of Guinea industry demands — producing engineers who don't just inspect the future, but compute and construct it."

Welcome to the Centre for Marine and Offshore Technology Development (CMOTD) at Rivers State University. Since our charter in 2021, our mandate has been unequivocal: to dismantle the historic barrier between university theoretical study and the high-precision software simulation suites used in the global offshore energy and computational maritime sectors.

Located in Port Harcourt — Nigeria’s energy and maritime nerve centre — our dual-pillar curriculum trains engineers not only in API and ASME code compliance, AVEVA E3D 3D routing, and Caesar II pipeline stress diagnostics, but equally equips them with full-stack software architecture, containerization, and predictive telemetry AI.

Whether you are an aspiring engineering graduate, an experienced practitioner upgrading to dynamic offshore simulations, or an enterprise seeking sovereign technical capability for local content mandates, CMOTD is your trusted academic partner.
