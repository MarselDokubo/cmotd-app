"Equipping African Engineers with Sovereign Computational Capability for the Global Energy Corridor"

For decades, the subsea engineering and deepwater offshore operations in the Gulf of Guinea relied heavily on external computational modeling, structural verification from distant engineering hubs, and expatriate technical sign-offs. At CMOTD, our mandate is uncompromising: to dismantle this technological dependency through uncompromising academic rigor and hands-on operational mastery.

Working directly under the auspices of the Rivers State University Faculty of Engineering, we have united traditional marine hydrodynamics and pipeline integrity engineering with high-velocity computer science. Our cohorts do not just study theoretical stress equations—they validate them in Caesar II, construct digital twins using AVEVA E3D, develop automation scripts in Python, and simulate dynamic multi-phase flows with Aspen HYSYS.

Whether you are an aspiring naval architect, an experienced offshore structural lead, or a software engineer building predictive maintenance algorithms for subsea manifolds, CMOTD provides the validated benchmark of excellence that multinational operators trust without reservation.

Evolutionary Roadmap

Strategic Objectives & Institutional Milestones

Since our chartering in 2021, CMOTD has systematically expanded laboratory capacity, accredited curriculum, and enterprise-grade software certifications.

Q1 2021
Charter Established

Foundation at RSU Faculty of Engineering

Ratified by the Rivers State University Senate and Governing Council as an autonomous centre of excellence dedicated to subsea development, offshore engineering dynamics, and computational systems development in Port Harcourt.

Q3 2022
Infrastructure Expansion

Commissioning of AVEVA E3D & Caesar II CAD Suites

Inauguration of the 60-seat High-Performance Engineering Workstation Suite, powered by AVEVA PDMS/E3D, Hexagon Caesar II stress analysis, and Aspen HYSYS simulation environments for real-world offshore projects.

Q2 2023
Curriculum Diversification

Rollout of Software Development & Industrial Machine Learning Tracks

Formal integration of cloud engineering (AWS/Azure), Python numerical scientific stack, and predictive machine learning models for subsea asset integrity, vessel tracking, and SCADA IoT data aggregation.

2024-2025

NCDMB Local Content Accreditation & Cryptographic Verification Registry

Achieved category-A certification with the Nigerian Content Development and Monitoring Board (NCDMB), alongside deployment of the public Cryptographic Certificate Verification Registry for international employer verification.
