Automation, Instrumentation & Control

Automation and instrumentation are central to modern industrial operations. This programme provides practical training in industrial automation, measurement, control systems, PLCs, DCS, HMI/SCADA, industrial networks, safety and cybersecurity. Participants progress from fundamental concepts to programming, process instrumentation, control-system design, industrial communication, robotics and practical automation projects, with exposure to IIoT, digital twins and AI/ML.

Training methodology

The programme combines theory, practical laboratory training, simulations, troubleshooting exercises, case studies and project-based learning. Participants work through industrial scenarios involving PLC programming, HMI/SCADA, instrument calibration, loop checks and control-loop tuning, culminating in a practical automation project integrating design, programming, testing and presentation.
