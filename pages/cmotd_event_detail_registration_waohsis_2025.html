Context & Strategic Objectives

The WAOHSIS-2025 symposium stands as West Africa’s premier offshore technical gathering, convened under the academic custody of the Centre for Marine and Offshore Technology Development (CMOTD) at Rivers State University. As exploitation accelerates in ultra-deepwater concessions across the Gulf of Guinea (water depths exceeding 1,800m), operators face compounded risks: extreme ocean current turbulence, seabed mudslides, hydrate plug formation in flowlines, and structural fatigue along dynamic subsea risers.

This 3-day high-density symposium brings international naval architects, computational fluid dynamics (CFD) specialists, field operators, and maritime regulatory bodies together to benchmark next-generation engineering strategies:

check_circle Validation of multiphase flow models using industrial Aspen HYSYS Digital Twins.
check_circle Towing tank metrology calibration for floating production storage and offloading (FPSO) hulls.
check_circle Deepwater cathodic protection monitoring & ultrasonic thickness mapping via untethered AUVs.
check_circle Compliance harmonisation with NCDMB Nigerian Content Act and COREN Continuing Professional Development.
