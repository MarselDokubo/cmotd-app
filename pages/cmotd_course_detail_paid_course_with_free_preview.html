Frequently Asked Questions: Free Preview vs. Full Track

Answers regarding trial limitations, workstation reservations, and certificate conferral.

How long does my Free Preview access remain active? expand_more

The introductory preview provides 14 calendar days of uninterrupted access to Modules 1 and 2, along with downloadable pipe specifications and self-evaluation exercises. You can upgrade to the paid full track at any point during or after this window without forfeiting completed modules.

Do I receive a certificate for the Free Preview? expand_more

No. The Free Preview issues an informal "Audit Completion Transcript" summarizing your quiz score and hours logged. The official, Senate-approved Rivers State University Certificate of Competence with tamper-evident cryptographic verification ledger is conferred exclusively upon defense of the full Capstone offshore topside project in the paid track.

Can I complete the entire course remotely without coming to Port Harcourt? expand_more

The introductory Modules 1 & 2 are 100% online on-demand. However, Modules 3 through 5 are conducted on high-performance physical CAD workstations at the RSU campus to comply with AVEVA software institutional enterprise licensing terms. For corporate groups residing outside Rivers State, an intensive 2-week residential executive residency option is available upon request.

What are the installment terms for the full NGN 350,000 tuition? expand_more

Trainees may choose either a single full payment (which guarantees workstation hardware assignment immediately) or a 2-part installment plan (60% / NGN 210,000 at enrollment to unlock Module 3, and the final 40% / NGN 140,000 prior to Module 5 Isometric extraction and capstone defense).
