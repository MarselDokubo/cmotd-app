Rigorous mechanical sizing, wall thickness calculations, collapse under external hydrostatic head, local buckling, on-bottom stability analysis, and expansion loop stress analysis using Caesar II and custom Python numerical routines.

  • check_circle Bursting limit state, propagating buckle, and buckle arrestor design

Dynamic modeling of Steel Catenary Risers (SCRs), Lazy Wave Risers, flexible flowlines, and electro-hydraulic control umbilicals attached to FPSO turret and spread-moored vessels.

Core Hydro-structural Topics
  • check_circle Top tension, touch-down zone (TDZ) bending moments & fatigue life
