"Bridging Heavy Maritime Engineering with Future-Ready Computational Intelligence"

Welcome to the Centre for Marine and Offshore Technology Development (CMOTD) at Rivers State University. When we established this Centre in 2021, our mandate was unequivocal: to eliminate the critical talent deficit in the Gulf of Guinea’s offshore energy corridor by equipping African engineers with world-class, computationally rigorous technical capabilities.

Today, the maritime industry has converged irreversibly with software architecture, finite element modeling, and real-time sensor analytics. Whether you are mastering 3D piping flexibility in Caesar II, modeling multi-phase thermodynamics in Aspen HYSYS, or developing distributed predictive maintenance applications, CMOTD offers the exact laboratory conditions and mentorship required to thrive globally.
