The PGD-SUB-801 curriculum is co-instructed by tenured engineering chairpersons from Rivers State University and certified Senior Technical Authorities (STAs) from active Niger Delta deepwater operations. Candidates are assigned into 1:4 engineering squads overseen directly by a Fellow of the Nigerian Society of Engineers (FNSE) and an offshore operator advisor.
