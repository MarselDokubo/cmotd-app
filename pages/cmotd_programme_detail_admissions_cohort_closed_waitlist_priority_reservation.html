Complete Programme Syllabus (60 CEU)

TRIMESTER 1 • 20 CREDITS

Subsea Production Architecture & Riser Hydraulics

Foundations

Deepwater field architectures, subsea trees (wet vs. dry), manifolds, templates, flowline jumpers, control umbilicals, and transient multiphase flow calculations under high-pressure, high-temperature (HPHT) regimes.

Core Tools: OLGA Multiphase Aspen HYSYS Python NumSci
TRIMESTER 2 • 20 CREDITS

Pipe Stress Analysis, Vortex-Induced Vibration & OrcaFlex Dynamics

Advanced Simulation

Comprehensive pipe stress compliance (ASME B31.8 / B31.4), static and dynamic analysis of steel catenary risers (SCR), flexible flowlines, slugging vibration attenuation, and hydrodynamic current load simulation.

Core Tools: Caesar II v13 OrcaFlex 11.4 AVEVA E3D Marine
TRIMESTER 3 • 20 CREDITS

Deepwater Pipeline Integrity, Subsea Inspection & Capstone Defense

Defense & Field Work

Corrosion management, intelligent pigging interpretation, cathodic protection design (DNV-RP-F103), autonomous underwater vehicle (AUV) inspection protocols, followed by an industrial capstone reviewed by an IOC expert panel.

Core Tools: ANSYS Mechanical RSU Towing Basin DNV Phast
