Entry Requirements & Candidate Eligibility

RSU Postgraduate School stringent vetting guidelines for engineering professionals.

Academic Credentials

Minimum of a Second Class Lower (2.2) Bachelor's Degree (B.Eng / B.Sc) in Mechanical, Marine, Civil, Chemical, or Petroleum Engineering from an NUC/COREN accredited institution, or recognized international equivalent.

Technical Prerequisites

Demonstrated foundational understanding of Fluid Mechanics, Strength of Materials, and Vector Calculus. Basic working familiarity with general 2D/3D CAD drafting tools is required prior to orientation.

Professional Experience

Minimum of 1 year of active post-NYSC engineering industry experience. Experienced candidates with HND (Upper Credit) plus 5+ years of verified offshore engineering practice may be considered via special Senate waiver.

Corporate Sponsorships

Corporate nominations by IOCs (Shell, TotalEnergies, Chevron, ExxonMobil) or major EPCI contractors (Saipem, Subsea7, TechnipFMC) receive expedited admissions review upon presentation of official company endorsement.

Faculty Leadership & Advisory Panel

Guided by senior marine academicians and active offshore deepwater consultants.
