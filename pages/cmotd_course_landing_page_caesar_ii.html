Engineering Integrity for Deepwater West Africa

In the Gulf of Guinea, offshore pipelines and steel catenary risers (SCRs) are subjected to extreme thermal gradients, internal multiphase slug dynamics, and cyclical vortex-induced vibrations caused by complex subsea currents. Structural failure at high pressure results not only in catastrophic environmental impact, but in multi-million dollar production shutdowns.

This intensive certification module equips pipeline integrity engineers, naval architects, and mechanical design specialists with direct proficiency in Hexagon Caesar II. Engineers transition from simple 2D beam approximations to complete non-linear 3D spatial stress analysis, including soil-pipe friction modeling, expansion loop optimization, flange leakage evaluation under ASME Section VIII, and seabed upheaval buckling mitigation.
