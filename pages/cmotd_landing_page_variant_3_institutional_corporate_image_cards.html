On behalf of the Governing Council and Management of Rivers State University, I welcome you to the Centre for Marine and Offshore Technology Development (CMOTD). Since our establishment in 2021, our mandate has been unequivocal: to bridge the critical gulf between academic theory and the high-rigor, industrial computational demands of international offshore exploration, subsea pipeline transmission, and modern software engineering.

Whether validating pressure vessels with ASME codes or fine-tuning neural networks for acoustic cavitation telemetry, CMOTD trainees operate on real enterprise toolchains: AVEVA E3D, Aspen HYSYS, Caesar II, and modern cloud platforms. We do not just certify; we forge technical leadership that stands proudly on the world stage.
