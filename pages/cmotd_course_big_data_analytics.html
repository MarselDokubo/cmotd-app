Big Data Analytics

Big Data Analytics is a practical professional training programme designed to equip participants with the knowledge and technical skills to collect, prepare, analyse, visualise and interpret data for effective decision-making. Participants move through the complete data analytics lifecycle and gain hands-on experience with Excel, SQL, Power BI, Python, Hadoop and Spark using real-world datasets and industry applications.
