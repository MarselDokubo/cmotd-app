Academic Scope

Course Overview & Learning Objectives

Nigeria's offshore oil and gas basin represents one of the most prolific yet safety-critical maritime environments globally. Designed specifically by senior academic engineers and marine surveyors at CMOTD, FND-101 provides an accessible, rigorous grounding in the statutory legal architecture, risk appraisal methodologies, and international environmental conventions governing Gulf of Guinea facilities.

gavel

Statutory Regulatory Regimes

Examine the legal mechanics of the IMO International Maritime Conventions: SOLAS chapters, MARPOL Annexes I-VI, and ISPS maritime facility security.

oil_barrel

Offshore Facility Architecture

Distinguish floating production storage and offloading (FPSO), fixed jacket platforms, TLPs, and spar structural components and safety zones.

flag

NOGICD Act Compliance

Navigate the Nigerian Oil & Gas Industry Content Development Act mandates for equipment fabrication, engineering person-hours, and local marine vessels.

health_and_safety

Permit-to-Work & HAZOP

Perform fundamental Hazard and Operability (HAZOP) studies, Job Safety Analyses (JSA), and hot/cold work permit reviews for live offshore decks.

Frequently Asked Questions

help_outline Why is this course 100% free with no hidden charges?

FND-101 is fully funded under the joint human capital capacity-building initiative between the Nigerian Content Development and Monitoring Board (NCDMB) and Rivers State University. Its primary goal is to democratize safety engineering competencies among Nigerian university students and entry-level offshore workers.

help_outline Do I receive an authentic, employer-verifiable certificate?

Yes. Upon achieving at least 80% on the final assessment, an official Rivers State University CMOTD Certificate of Completion is issued with a permanent cryptographic SHA-256 hash and unique QR code searchable directly on the university's public verification ledger.

help_outline Can I upgrade from this free course to hands-on physical CAD cohorts?

Absolutely. Completing FND-101 satisfies the safety prerequisite for our professional certificate programs in AVEVA E3D/PDMS, Caesar II Piping Stress Analysis, and Aspen HYSYS process simulation conducted in our CADICAE Training Center in Port Harcourt.
