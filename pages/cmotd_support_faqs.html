Institutional Knowledge Base

Frequently Answered Protocols

Detailed answers curated directly by CMOTD faculty, software administrators, and university registrars.

01 What qualifications do I need to enroll in the Subsea Pipeline Analysis (Caesar II) course?
expand_more

Applicants must possess at minimum a Bachelor of Engineering (B.Eng), B.Sc, or Higher National Diploma (HND) in Mechanical, Marine, Civil, Petroleum, or Chemical Engineering from an accredited tertiary institution.

Prior foundational understanding of fluid mechanics, mechanics of materials, and baseline 2D/3D CAD drafting is strongly recommended. Trainees without finite element analysis background are mandated to attend our complimentary 3-day Applied Engineering Numerical Refresher module prior to cohort launch.

info Corporate sponsored candidates may submit employer recommendation letters in lieu of certain academic prerequisites.
02 How can foreign or international trainees obtain visa documentation for on-campus simulation labs at Port Harcourt?
expand_more

Upon acceptance and receipt of the primary tuition installment, the CMOTD Secretariat liaises with the Rivers State University Directorate of International Affiliations to issue an official Subject to Regularization (STR) Visa Endorsement & Invitation Dossier.

This dossier includes proof of laboratory registration, security clearance with the Port Harcourt Joint Marine Task Force, and verified on-campus or partner executive hotel accommodations. International trainees must allow a minimum of 4 weeks for consulate clearance prior to physical class start.

03 Are CMOTD certifications recognized by international operators and local regulatory bodies (NCDMB, COREN)?
expand_more

Yes, unconditionally. CMOTD curricula are developed under direct technical partnerships with leading software vendors (AVEVA, Hexagon, AspenTech) and fully audited by the Council for the Regulation of Engineering in Nigeria (COREN) and the Nigerian Content Development and Monitoring Board (NCDMB).

Each accredited certificate awards official Continuing Engineering Education (CEU) credits recognizable across IOCs (Shell, TotalEnergies, Chevron, ExxonMobil, NLNG) operating in the Gulf of Guinea and international maritime jurisdictions.

04 Can corporate energy operators sponsor private cohort schedules for AVEVA E3D or Aspen HYSYS?
expand_more

Yes. The CMOTD Executive Training Unit accommodates custom, closed-cohort training schedules tailored specifically to engineering contractor project specifications (e.g., greenfield FPSO design, subsea tie-back modeling).

Corporate programs can be scheduled either at our state-of-the-art dual-monitor simulation suites in Port Harcourt or deployed directly to client facilities equipped with mobile licensed dongle servers. Please contact our Industry Hub desk for dedicated group quotation schedules.

05 How does the instant cryptographic QR certificate verification process work?
expand_more

Every certificate issued by CMOTD since 2021 contains an embedded SHA-256 cryptographic security hash and a unique QR locator pointing directly to our immutable registry servers at Rivers State University.

Third-party recruiters and verification officers can scan the QR code or enter the certificate alphanumeric serial (e.g., RSU-CMOTD-2025-CE-8841) in the public verification portal to inspect real-time candidate name, module grades, CEU units, issue timestamps, and electronic registrar signatures.

06 What happens if a simulation testbed session is rescheduled due to campus maintenance?
expand_more

All physical workstation reservations are backed by our dual-redundant backup power systems and automated virtual environment checkpoints. In the rare event of campus-wide mechanical maintenance or network provisioning:

Trainees receive SMS/Email notifications at least 24 hours in advance, and all affected workstation lab credits are automatically converted to equal-duration cloud-streamed remote GPU terminal sessions with 14-day archival access to save project states.
